## ingredients

### syrup

* 355ml water
* 300g sugar
* 80ml orange juice
* 1 cinnamon stick

### cake

* 450 grams filo sheets
* 4 eggs
* 150g sugar
* zest from two oranges
* 230g plain greek yoghurt
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
* 1 teaspoon baking powder
* 1 teaspoon baking soda
* &frac14; teaspoon salt
* 235ml vegetable oil
* 120ml orange juice

## method

1. Start by preparing the syrup.  Combine the water, sugar, orange juice and the cinnamon stick in a pan and bring to the boil, then simmer uncovered for 15 minutes.  Remove from heat and let cool.
2. Dry out the filo sheets by placing in an oven at 100&deg;C / 200&deg;F. Open up the sheets, and loosely scrunch them up, starting from the short side. Place the sheets on a baking tray. Bake in the middle and bottom racks of the oven for 10 minutes, then flip the sheets over and bake for an additional 8 minutes. Turn off the oven and leave the sheets to dry with the oven door slightly open. Once completely dry crumble the sheets into small pieces, and set them aside.
3. Preheat the oven to 180&deg;C / 350&deg;F / gas 4.
4. Combine the eggs and the sugar in a large mixing bowl and beat for 3 – 4 minutes, until it is a pale yellow colour.
5. Add the orange zest, yoghurt, vanilla, baking powder, baking soda and salt, and mix until just combined.
6. Add the oil and the orange juice to the bowl, and mix well.
7. Incorporate your dried out and torn filo into the cake batter a little bit at a time to ensure the pastry pieces doesn't clump together.
8. Pour the mixture into a greased baking pan and bake for 50-60 minutes in the middle of the oven until a nice golden colour.
9. Remove from the oven and immediately pierce it in several places with a long clean skewer, then pour the cooled syrup onto the hot cake, one ladle at at time.
10. Let your cake cool before cutting, to allow the syrup to be fully absorbed.
