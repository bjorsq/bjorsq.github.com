## ingredients

### syrup

* 350ml water
* 300g sugar
* 80ml orange juice
* 1 cinnamon stick

### cake

* 500g filo sheets
* 4 eggs
* 150g sugar
* zest from four oranges
* 230g plain greek yoghurt
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
* 1 teaspoon baking powder
* 1 teaspoon baking soda
* 230g salted butter
* 120ml orange juice

## method

1. Zest four oranges and squeeze them - you should end up with about 200ml of juice.
2. Combine the water, sugar, orange juice and the cinnamon stick in a pan and bring to the boil, then simmer the syrup uncovered for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let cool.
3. Dry out the filo sheets by placing in an oven at 100&deg;C / 200&deg;F. Loosely scrunch the sheets up, starting from the short side, and place the sheets on baking trays. Bake in the middle and bottom racks of the oven for 10-15 minutes, then flip the sheets over and bake for an additional 10-15 minutes. Remove them from the oven and leave them to dry.
4. Preheat the oven to 180&deg;C / 350&deg;F / gas 4.
5. Combine the eggs and the sugar in a large mixing bowl and beat for 3 – 4 minutes, until it is a pale yellow colour.
6. Add the orange zest, yoghurt, vanilla, baking powder and baking soda - mix until combined.
7. Melt the butter in a pan and add the orange juice. Add to the bowl when cooled and mix well.
8. Crush and tear the dried out filo into the cake batter a sheet at a time, mixing to ensure the pastry pieces doesn't clump together.
9. Pour the mixture into a large greased baking dish and bake for 50-60 minutes in the middle of the oven until a nice golden colour.
10. Remove from the oven and immediately pierce it in with a cocktail stick, then pour the cooled syrup onto the hot cake.
11. Let the cake cool before cutting, to allow the syrup to be fully absorbed.
